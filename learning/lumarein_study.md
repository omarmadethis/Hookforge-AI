## Why it went viral (vs our playbook)
1. **#1 is a then-vs-now DIY-fail hook** (duct-tape LED strip). It has a clear point of conflict at frame 0, it's absurd and relatable, and the VS card pays off. It got 6.8x the next one, with a share rate of 1.1% and a like rate of 8.8%, which is huge. This matches our F06 and the problem-hook format.
2. **Identity callouts** ("equestrian", "horse girlie", "horseback rider") plus forbidden-knowledge framing ("Do NOT show this to…"), with sends/shares as the goal. That is the consumer-psych tribe/identity and taboo-curiosity levers.
3. **Proven POC hooks only**: store hook (#3, #4), covered reveal (#2) and 3-2-1 with "let's see how it comes out" (#5). This validates our hook-format list.
4. **The visual payoff is intrinsically shareable**: neon glow on a black-night horse plus the reflection shot, and the switch-on "click" moment gives instant transformation.
5. **Hard cuts every 1–2 s**, the after is 3 hard-cut shots (beach, ear POV, group), the song starts at frame 0, it's 13–15 s, and the card sits at the top centre, at 8 words or fewer.
6. The CTA uses "Comment" (TikTok). Our FB rule is SAY.
- Caveat: they reuse identical body footage across all 12 reels and only swap hooks, and TikTok didn't punish it here. **Our rule stays: every Hookforge video is 100% unique footage.**
