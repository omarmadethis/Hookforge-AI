# Shot grammar: angles, distances and sequences that win (2026-10-18 PT)
**Method.** For each posted reel joined to a CapCut draft, I pulled one frame at the midpoint of every CapCut video segment (so shots are the team's real cuts, not a detector's guesses). For the top external reels I used one frame per sidecar shot, or one every 1.5 s. Every shot was hand-coded from the frames (`_work/shots/SHEET_*.jpg`). Data: `shots.csv` (376 shots, 52 reels: 41 ours with views, 11 external ≥3.5M), `shots_by_reel.csv` (rebuilt by `shots_by_reel.py`), code `shot_codes.py`. **Covered-reveal audit (added 2026-10-18 ~10 PM PT):** 12 more reels coded after Omar flagged that the covered-reveal winners were missing. That includes 3 IG-only reels downloaded read-only at low res. Files: `covered_hooks.csv`, `covered_hooks.py`, `covered_stats.json`; frames in `_work/covered/`. Prompt phrasings come from `shot_prompt_phrasings.csv` (prompts ledger; only 51 prompts / 15 reels are confirmed in posted reels, so the phrasing evidence is thin).
Codes: angle (eye/high/low/pov/ots/top) · distance (ECU/CU/MCU/MS/WS) · handheld/static · subject (product/person/pop = product-on-person/env) · motion · product screen share · face visible · setting. Single coder; the codes are hand-checked frame by frame (100% of coded rows were viewed).

## Headline findings (ours: winners ≥50K n=16, flops <10K n=18, mid n=6; the MF-02/MJIG-04 same edit is counted once)
1. **The covered-reveal hook is a proven hook (Omar, 2026-10-18 9:55 PM PT; the earlier "0/5, block it" finding was wrong because the robe winners weren't coded).** At 0 s a person holds the product under or as a cover (a tray, muffin tin, cloth or drape), then a 3-2-1 / pull reveal. Across all our reels it went **5/16 ≥50K**: HJ-02 **2.7M**, HJ-05 **524K**, MJIG-17 **241K** (IG), HJ-10 **147K**, HJ-14 **97K**. On the robe (product held constant) it's **5/11 (45%) vs 21/48 (44%)** for other robe hooks, with a higher median (28.9K vs 15.7K). Fisher p=1.0, so it performs on par with our other hooks, not worse.
   **What separates covered-reveal winners from flops:**
   - **(a) The product shows through the cover at 0 s. This is the main split.** Either the product itself is visible around the cover (lime fur, the Grinch heart or face under the muffin tin or tray, the robe shaped into a "loaf"), or the drape is the product's own fabric and colour (lime fur or Grinch-green drape over a store display). **Cue visible: 5/10 ≥50K, median 63K. Fully hidden or mismatched: 0/6, median 3.0K** (Fisher p=0.09). The fully hidden flops are black cloth HJ-25 6.2K; plaid blanket or steel pot over the ski mask (MJIG-08 3.3K, MJIG-09 2.7K, MJIG-12 1.1K); cream cloth over the fire pit MGS-09 457. The fire pit under a green robe-looking cloth (MJIG-04 9.0K = MF-02 3.1K) has the wrong cue: it reads as the robe, with no flame or glow.
   - **(b) The reveal lands by 3.0 s.** All 5 winners revealed at 1.2–3.0 s; only 5 of the 11 non-winners did (others 3.2–4.5 s).
   - **(c) Size and camera.** In the tray/tin version, the product fills 35–60% of the frame in a static, eye-level MS. HJ-22 (16K) had the same loaf at 15% in a WS. The store-unveil version is a static WS with a staff line-up, and the drape comes off by about 2.3 s.
   - **(d) Setting.** Winners were shot at home (bedroom or living room, tree, warm light) or in a retail store with staff. Every outdoor or snow covered-reveal flopped, but all of those were also fully hidden, so this is confounded with (a).
   - **Caveats.** All 5 winners are the heated robe, so a cue-visible covered reveal hasn't been tried on another product yet. Cue-visible misses: HJ-26 5.9K and IG Dcw9GXbK6u7 3.6K were page-launch-week posts (Sep 4 FB and Sep 1 IG, when the other posts also got 3–6K). HJ-29 4.6K was a near-copy of HJ-02 posted 2 days later, with a non-English VO and a 4.0 s reveal. IG DdDqyRKqDQc 28.9K was handheld, with a 3.5 s reveal.
   **Engine:** allowed, normal hook (D52). Requirements: a visible product cue under or around the cover at 0 s, or a drape in the product's own colour or fabric; reveal by 3.0 s; static eye-level MS (tray) or WS staff unveil (drape); a 3-2-1 VO is fine.
   **Non-robe products (hypothesis, innovation slot):** the cue has to be that product's own signal. For the fire pit, a warm glow or flame tip showing under the lifted cloth edge. For the ski mask, the yellow smiley peeking out.
2. **Then-vs-now winners open on a wide, chaotic, product-less "then" shot.** The "then" is a WS handheld household disaster (tangled lights, a ladder fall, a smoke-filled room, a competitor pit flaring), then a POV/top-down unbox, then a static WS/CU hero of the lit product. MG-01 152.9K, MG-06 70.4K, MGS-01 67.7K, MJIG-01 40.3K(IG), MJIG-02 30.9K(IG). The one F04 flop (MGS-06 2.6K) opened on a mid-shot dinner-table fire with faces, and the rest of it used the face-forward demo grammar below. "Product at 0 s" is not required for F04: 4 of 5 coded F04 winners (MG-01, MG-06, MGS-01, MJIG-01) open without the new product. MG-04's first coded shot is a POV unbox, but its segment midpoints were sparse.
3. **Hands-on-product angles beat a face-forward presenter (same product, MerryFire, n=12).** The ≥30K fire-pit reels (67.7K, 63.0K, 40.3K, 30.9K) have 14–43% of shots at a **high angle looking down at hands on the product** (OTS or across the table). Face share is ≤0.43, except the FaceTime 35K at 0.71. Every <10K fire-pit reel is an eye-level MS of a woman demoing to camera: face share 0.44–0.88, high-angle share ≤0.13. Across all our reels the direction is the same (winners face share 0.25 vs flops 0.39, person-subject 0.19 vs 0.38) but not significant at n=25 (p=0.30, 0.18). **Engine: at most 1 face-forward demo shot. Body shots are POV, OTS or high-angle hands plus product; people show up as reactions in MS or WS.**
4. **A product-on-person "display" hook is 3/3** (HJ-13 98K, MC-01 63.3K, MC-02 62.8K): the person holds or wears the lit or worn product, centred, static, MS. External mega-hits use the same move: the princess robe held up flat, then worn, then POV close-ups of the heat pad and controller (26M and 15.8M).
5. **The last shot is a static hero of the lit or worn product (CU/WS, product share 0.6–0.7).** It appears on winners and flops alike, so it's table stakes, not a lever.
6. **BrickHood proves shots aren't the whole story.** BH-02 (61.1K, FB) and MJIG-10 (2.6K, IG) use the **same shot sequence**: POV chairlift product hold → low-angle wear → squad on lift → snow angel → ski action → après beers. Distribution/platform explains that gap, which matches the backtester's page-momentum finding.
7. **External ≥3.5M reels (n=11) use three shot grammars:** (a) hands-only CU, POV or high angle on the product (CarPlay 8.5M, shelf liner 6.9M, projector light 23.7M: plug-in CU → room-filling WS effect); (b) product-on-person display → wear → POV feature CUs (robes 26M/15.8M, heated jacket 6.8M: cold-fail MS → reveal → wear); (c) a face-forward talking presenter (photo printer 3.8M, store 3.5M). (c) is creator-led, and that's not our AI-UGC model.

## Seedance prompt phrasings that produced these angles (from posted 30K+ prompts)
Use them verbatim inside the packed-gen format (`SHOT 1 (0.0–2.0s)… HARD CUT at 2.0s. SHOT 2 (2.0–4.0s), new <angle>…`), always ending with the iPhone 12 suffix (D32).
| target shot | phrasing (source reel, views) |
|---|---|
| High-angle / OTS hands on product | "Handheld phone video **over her shoulder**, natural real-time speed, quick casual movements, no slow motion, no cuts." (MGS-01, 67.7K) |
| Low-angle ignite across the table | "SHOT 2 (2.0–4.0s), **new low angle from across the coffee table facing the couch**: she touches the … lighter to the rim of the cup and real orange flames whoosh up" (MF-01, 63.0K); "new low angle from across the table at fire-bowl height" (MGS-03, 35.4K) |
| Static hero close-up with a slow push | "**cinematic low-angle close-up** of the [product] on its snack tray on the same coffee table, **slow push-in**: the flame dances in the steel cup, Christmas tree lights … behind" (MF-01, 63.0K; MGS-03, 35.4K) |
| Wide "then" chaos | "Realistic handheld iPhone footage **filmed from across the room**, very slight natural phone shake, no cinematic grading, no slow motion" (MG-01 152.9K, MG-06 70.4K) + "Settling shot from the still-shaking phone: the dad lies half-buried under the tipped tree" (MG-01) |
| Lit-product hero, low side angle | "**Medium shot from a lower angle off to the side**, closer to the tree, framing the tree from base to star with part of the sofa and the American flag in the dark background." (MG-01, 152.9K) |
| Table-height product close | "new **close angle at table height** on the same snowy patio table: the round … tray … sits on the table and her hands set…" (MGS-03, 35.4K) |
| POV chairlift product hold (BrickHood) | "**first-person POV** on a moving chairlift, **looking down at two grey-gloved hands (exactly two hands)** … holding a sealed c[lear bag]" (BrickHood start-frame prompt in the ledger; not confirmed in a posted reel, but it is exactly the BH-02/BH-03 opener) |
| Candid, not presenter | "Ordinary family, **nobody looks at the camera**" (60K+ MerryFire/JollyGlow prompts, Mac REPORT §2). Counter-check: in the ledger, prompts with "not looking at the camera" sit on posted reels with median 2.9K, because they were paired with "huge candid shock" faces. Keep candid, never shocked |
**Don't use:** security-cam framing as a hook; "front angle" presenter shots for more than 1 slot; "selfie" except BrickHood squad selfies (MCU, goggles on).

## For the engine (`planner.py` / `qa.py`)
- Covered-reveal hooks are **allowed** (D52, Omar 2026-10-18 9:55 PM PT). QA advisory (`covered_cue`): at 0 s the product's own cue (colour/texture/face/glow) must show under or around the cover, or the drape must be the product's own colour or fabric. Reveal by 3.0 s. No blocking, no experiment-slot restriction.
- Body plan: ≥2 of the body shots are `pov|ots|high` with hands on the product; ≤1 face-forward demo shot; reactions in MS/WS. Each packed gen's 2nd shot uses a **new angle** (Omar R08).
- F04: hook = WS handheld chaos without the new product; payoff = static hero. Product at 0 s isn't required for F04 (it's still required for other formats per the D35 deviation check).
- World guard: the ski product's hook setting ∈ {chairlift, slope, gondola, snowmobile}; never store or indoor.
