## 1. What it is, and why it works (evidence-backed)
Talent holds the product disguised as something else (folded on a baking tray, under a muffin tin, under a cloth) and counts '3, 2, 1… let's see how it comes out' in native AI audio, then flips/tosses/unveils the product on 'one'. The nonsense is the hook; the countdown gives the viewer a reason to stay to ~3 s.

- FB hit rate with the VO: 11/18 ≥50K (61%) vs HJ base rate median ~59K (hooks_features vo_321 column).
- Decisive condition: product visible at 0.0 s. Every 3-2-1 reel with product_pct_0 = 0 flopped (IG 9K, 3.3K, 1.1K; HJ 6.2K; MerryFires 3.1K; Merry Glows 457), while 11/15 with the product visible won (hook_features.csv product_pct_0).
