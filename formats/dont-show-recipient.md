## 1. What it is, and why it works (evidence-backed)
A forbidden-secret line naming the gift RECIPIENT, held over a first frame where the product is big and legible (≥30% of frame), followed by the standard 5-shot try-on/activation body. The line casts the viewer as the buyer hiding a gift; the footage carries the views.

- Second-biggest family on HeatedJammies: 2.7M, 183K, 165K, 112K, 98K, 97K, 88K, all fresh posts (hooks REPORT §4 G2/G3; PLAYBOOK §1.1).
- Biggest external confirmation in the whole dataset: thecozygrump.us 'DO NOT show this to your mom😱😂' on a heated Grinch robe, 12.4M plays / 141.3K shares, posted the same week as our 2.7M; same hook re-rendered 1.2M; merry.made2 1.4M (external REPORT §2).
- Framing, not wording, decides it: same text 'DO NOT let your mom see this ❄️😳' got 2.7M with the loaf at 35% of frame vs 16K with a full-body woman and the loaf at 15% (hooks REPORT §1.3, natural_experiments.csv).
- Recipient word is required but not sufficient: 'DONT show this to a Christmas MOM' over a Times Square tree with no product at 0 s got 4.2K (PLAYBOOK §1.1).

## 10. Original-variant rules (experiments, ≤20% of output)
- **Kids-recipient decor.** On light-up decor: 'DO NOT let your {grandkids/KIDS} see this..👀🎄' over a POV unpack of the product already half-lit by 2 s. _Proof of concept:_ zombie.crawler 3.2M; 55-64/65+ are the top Insights ages on Merry Glow (PLAYBOOK §2), so grandkids is the buyer's recipient. _Kill/promote:_ 3 renders; kill if all < page median at 72 h, promote if any ≥3× median (external REPORT §5).
- **Store-shelf don't-show.** Swap H1 for a shocked shopper pointing at a shelf of the branded boxes; body unchanged. _Proof of concept:_ HJ 183K (grandma gasp) + merryglow.official 198K + tryglowmas 169K/159K. _Kill/promote:_ Same 3-render rule; the shopper must react by 2 s (SHOCK_STORE median is 6.2K without a striking reaction).
